Our Story

From Bacoli to the Jersey Shore

Gennaro Costigliola was sixteen when he left Bacoli — a small town on the Bay of Naples — carrying thirty years of family cooking ahead of him before he even knew it. The recipes came with him: pasta rolled by hand, sauces that are never rushed, seafood cooked the way it's cooked along that coast.

Today, alongside his wife Danielle, he brings that same Neapolitan table to Toms River. Stop by most evenings and you'll find him doing what he's always done — walking the room, checking on every table, making sure the night feels the way it did back home.
