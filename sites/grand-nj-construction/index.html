What we do

Five trades. One phone call.

Most of this work overlaps. A chimney leak soaks the roof deck, a bad gutter rots the siding, and settled brick cracks everything above it. Getting one crew who does all of it means nobody gets to point at somebody else.

  1. 01

    Chimney

    Crowns, caps, loose or missing brick, water finding its way down the flue. The thing most people only look at once it's already staining a ceiling.

  2. 02

    Roofing

    Full replacements and repairs. Shingles, flashing, valleys, the places water actually gets in — which is almost never where the stain shows up inside.

  3. 03

    Siding

    Vinyl and aluminum, whole houses or the one wall the weather has been working on for a decade.

  4. 04

    Gutters

    New runs, repairs, leaders and downspouts. Cheap to fix, expensive to ignore — overflow goes straight into the fascia and then the foundation.

  5. 05

    Masonry

    Steps, stoops, walls, repointing. Brick and block work that has to match what's already there, which is most of the job.
