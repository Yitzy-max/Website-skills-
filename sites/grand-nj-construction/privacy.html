Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 September 2026

Short version: this website doesn't track you. The only information we ever get is what you type into the form and send us, and we use it to get back to you about the work.

What we collect

Only what you hand over. If you fill in the inquiry form, that's your name, your phone number, your email address if you chose to give one, and whatever you wrote in the message box. If you sign up to the mailing list, that's your email address.

We don't collect anything if you just read the page. No account, no profile, no hidden form fields.

What we do with it

We use your details to answer you, to come out and look at the job, and to give you a price. That's it. We don't sell it, rent it, or hand it to marketing companies. We don't use it to advertise at you somewhere else.

If you signed up to the mailing list, we'll email you a few times a year about seasonal work. Every one of those emails has an unsubscribe link and using it is the end of it.

Who else sees it

We'd also have to hand something over if the law required it. That has never come up.

Cookies

This site sets none. Not one. There's no analytics, no tracking pixel, no advertising tag, no session cookie and no consent banner, because there's nothing to consent to. If your browser shows you a cookie list for this site, it should be empty.

Two things do load from other companies' servers, and they can see your IP address and browser details the same way any website you visit can:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) supplies the typefaces on the page.
  • Cloudflare's cdnjs (cdnjs.cloudflare.com) supplies the two small animation libraries that drive the scrolling. If it's unreachable the page falls back to a copy stored on this site instead.

Neither is used to identify you and neither sets a cookie on this site.

Children

This is a roofing and masonry business. The site isn't aimed at children and we don't knowingly collect anything from them.

Your choices

Ask us to show you what we hold, correct it, or delete it, and we will. Call 551-222-5512. [TO CONFIRM — a business email address for written requests.]
