Why you'll stay

Most coffee stops are built to get you out the door. This one isn't.

1/4

Work

Quiet enough to actually get things done.

Free wifi, outlets by the tables, and nobody hovering to turn the table. Mornings on Route 9 are slower than they look.

Kids

A toy corner, so you can finish your coffee.

There's a small room of toys and books for the little ones and a kids' drink menu so they get something of their own.

Outside

Two tables on the deck for the good mornings.

Bistro tables out front, sun until early afternoon, and a street that stays quiet.

Slow

Bring a book. Bring a date. Stay a while.

Sage walls, soft light, a low hum instead of a rush. The kind of room you don't feel the need to leave.
