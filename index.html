Three Reasons to Stay

Built for lingering, not just for lattes

Quiet corners for the ones working. A whole corner of toys for the kids. Board games at every table for everyone else. Caffeine Haven was built around staying, not rushing.

For the Deep-Focus Crowd

Order a second cup. Stay as long as you like.

It's quiet in the early morning, and it doesn't really pick up until around 10:30 — which leaves a long, calm window for laptops, long to-do lists, and a coffee that keeps up with the whole thing.

Kids Welcome

A whole corner for them. A quiet minute for you.

There's a dedicated play corner — toys and all — so the kids stay busy while you actually finish that conversation, or just finish your coffee while it's still hot.

Books, Dates & Slow Afternoons

There's always a board game out, if you want one.

Tucked into downtown Main Street, with a table free and a game within reach — it's where a first date turns into a second, and a quick stop turns into an afternoon.

The Room

Low light, good chairs, better coffee

A peek at the space — dim lighting, warm wood, and the small details that make it worth staying in.

Our Story

Built to be a reason to stay

Caffeine Haven opened in August 2024 in downtown Toms River, in a storefront that used to be a beloved local coffee stop. The goal wasn't just a good cup of coffee — it was somewhere people would actually want to stay.

So that's what's here: a quiet morning before the 10:30 rush, a corner of toys for the kids, and a stack of board games for whoever wants one. Work here, catch up with a friend here, or just get a coffee and a muffin and watch Main Street go by.
