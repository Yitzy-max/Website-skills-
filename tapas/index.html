Mostly appetizers. That's the point.

A table that keeps
filling up.

Nobody orders one thing here. You order six, they land one at a time, and the table rearranges itself around whatever just showed up. It's the kind of meal that runs long on purpose. Brisket, short rib, skewers off the grill, all of it meant to be reached across for.

Glatt kosher under KCL supervision, and BYOB, so bring the bottle you actually want to drink.

The ones people
come back for.

Ten or so plates rotate through the kitchen. These are the ones that leave the pass fastest.

The plates.

Everything is glatt kosher, meat, and made to share. The kitchen runs daily from five.

01

Short Rib Cigars

Shredded beef short rib rolled tight in a crisp wrapper, fried to order, roasted garlic aioli.

02

Tapas Flatbread

BBQ brisket over crisp in-house flatbread, fresh arugula, chipotle aioli.

03

BBQ Brisket Nachos

Slow-cooked brisket piled over corn chips with pico de gallo, guacamole and chipotle crema.

04

Mango Habanero Poppers

Chicken poppers in a mango habanero glaze. Sweet first, heat after.

05

Honey Buffalo Poppers

The same poppers under honey buffalo, with slaw on the side to cool it down.

06

BBQ Brisket Tacos

Three to an order. Brisket, shredded lettuce, pico, chipotle crema.

07

Grilled Steak Skewer

Marinated in house sauce, off the grill, served with a fresh salad.

08

Tapas Burger

Griddled burgers on soft buns, tomato and lettuce, fries alongside.

09

Mango Chicken

A house favorite. Reviewers call it out by name.

10

Cornflake Chicken Fingers

Crunchy cornflake crust, cut for sharing. The table's easiest yes.

The full menu runs longer than this and changes with the kitchen. Call for tonight's board, or ask about anything you don't see.

The room.

A dark, quiet dining room off Route 9, with enough space between tables that you can hear each other, and a kitchen that keeps sending things out while you talk.

Weekends fill up. Call ahead and the table will be waiting.
