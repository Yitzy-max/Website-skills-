# SnakeFit × BHS — Website Vision Brief

Source: `SnakeFit_BHS_Website_Vision_Brief.pdf` (client-provided, 4 pages). This is the text version so it's searchable and easy to reference while building.

## The big idea
A premium fitness website that feels as intuitive as shopping online. Visitors discover coaches, explore qualifications and personalities, review real results, compare services, and take the next step — all before entering the gym.

**Guiding principle:** We're not just advertising a gym. Each trainer gets a complete, credible profile so a prospect can pick the person they trust.

## 01 Client experience (the funnel)
1. **Discover** — watch the founder intro, understand the gym
2. **Explore** — browse four trainers, open detailed profiles
3. **Evaluate** — certifications, specialties, videos, transformations, feedback
4. **Choose** — pick a trainer, review relevant packages
5. **Take action** — book a free evaluation or buy a package where available

## 02 Homepage & founder video
First screen = high-quality founder video. Covers:
- Who we are / what SnakeFit / BHS represents
- Walkthrough of the gym and facilities
- What makes the coaching approach different
- Preview of services, packages, real training sessions
- Why to trust the team

**Homepage CTAs:** Meet Our Trainers · Explore Packages · Book a Free Evaluation

## 03 Trainer marketplace — four profiles (centerpiece)
Cards: professional photo, name, key specialty, brief credentials, "View Profile".

Full profile contains:
| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| Professional imagery | High-quality portrait + authentic training photos |
| Personal intro video | How the coach speaks, works, communicates, approaches training |
| Bio & background | Story, professional history, experience |
| Verified credentials | Certs, qualifications, education, achievements |
| Coaching specialties | Client goals and training areas |
| Client results | Real transformations, with client permission |
| Reviews & testimonials | Authentic client feedback |
| Packages & booking | Services, pricing, direct free-evaluation option |

**Most important profile asset:** the intro video — visitors should feel they know the trainer before meeting.

## 04 Packages & pricing (below trainers)
Each package shows price, what's included, who it's for, next step. Final pricing TBD.
- **One-on-One Training** — dedicated trainer, tailored program
- **Semi-Private Training** — small group, up to 3 people
- **Group Classes / Bootcamps** — structured group workouts with instruction
- **VIP Coaching** — with the founder: personalized training, nutrition guidance, progress tracking, ongoing support

## 05 Free evaluation & booking
Book without calling or messaging first. Two paths:
- **Path A — Trainer Evaluation:** pick one of the four trainers, book a free initial eval with them
- **Path B — VIP Evaluation:** free consult directly with the founder about VIP coaching

## 06 Shopping-style functionality
Browse cards → detailed profiles → package options → booking. "Add to Cart" + checkout for packages approved for direct purchase.
Recommended: real appointment availability, simple contact form, automated confirmations + reminders, clear next steps after booking.

## 07 Design & brand direction
Premium coaching, credibility, real results. Official SnakeFit + BHS branding, polished type, clean layouts, pro video, authentic gym photography. **Must be excellent on mobile.**
- **Look:** modern, high-end, clean, energetic, professional
- **Content:** real coaches, real facilities, authentic transformations, clear qualifications
- **Navigation:** simple paths to trainer profiles, packages, free evaluations
- **Trust:** verified credentials, consent-based transformation images, genuine testimonials
- **Performance:** fast pages, accessible text, responsive

## 08 Open decisions (client still to finalize)
- Which packages are buy-now vs. evaluation-first?
- Side-by-side trainer comparison?
- Separate live booking calendar per trainer?
- Client accounts for purchases/appointments?
- Which scheduling, checkout, payment systems?
- Final package names, prices, trainer bios, media assets

## Final creative direction
Not a standard gym brochure site. A premium, interactive coaching marketplace where visitors discover, evaluate, trust, select, and book their trainer with confidence.
