# Photos

Drop your photos in this folder using the exact filenames below. The site picks
them up with no other changes. Until a file is here, that frame renders as a
warm dark plate instead of a broken image, so the page still looks finished.

Shoot or pick warm, low-light, real photos. Phone photos are fine. Avoid
stock-looking shots and anything obviously generated.

| File | Where it shows | Best shape | What it should be |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `exterior-dusk.jpg` | Opening screen and the closing "Come in" section | Landscape, wide (3:2 or wider) | The storefront at dusk with the windows lit. Shot from the lot, a little distance, so the lit window sits around the middle of the frame. |
| `interior-door.jpg` | Opening screen (as you scroll in) and the Howell section | Landscape or square | The room seen from just inside the door. Tables, pendant lights, the corner chair. |
| `rain-window.jpg` | "Stay a while" — the tall image | Portrait (2:3) | The front window from a table inside. Rain, condensation, or just evening light through the glass. |
| `cup-table.jpg` | "Stay a while" — the small overlapping image | Portrait (3:4) | Close up: a cup and a pastry on a table. Crumbs and a used napkin are good. |
| `window-laptop.jpg` | "The window seat has an outlet" | Landscape, tall crop (4:3) | Someone working at the window in morning light. Shot from behind or the side, no posing. |
| `armchair-book.jpg` | "Nobody will ask what you're reading" | Portrait (2:3) | Hands and an open book in the armchair under the lamp. |
| `kids-corner.jpg` | "Bring them" | Landscape (3:2) | The kids' corner. Books, blocks, crayons, low table. |
| `out-front.jpg` | "Out front, when the weather's right" | Landscape, wide (16:9) | The sidewalk tables early in the day. A dog under a table is a bonus. |
| `pour.jpg` | The menu section, first image | Portrait (3:4) | Milk going into a cup at the counter. Close, hands in frame. |
| `pastry-case.jpg` | The menu section, second image | Portrait (3:4) | The pastry case, a few things already sold out. |
| `two-cups.jpg` | "Meet someone here" | Landscape (3:2) | Two cups on a small table by the window, afternoon light. |
| `owner-counter.jpg` | The Howell section | Portrait (3:4) | Whoever is behind the counter, mid-laugh, handing a cup across. |

Sizes: 2000px on the long edge is plenty. Save as JPG at about 80% quality and
keep each file under roughly 400KB so the page stays fast. If you'd rather use
`.webp` or `.avif`, change the filename extensions in `index.html` to match.
