Coffee, pastries, and a seat nobody is going to ask you to give up.

Come in

Route 9 is loud.
This isn't.

Come in.

Stay a while.

Nobody's turning tables. Order something, find a seat, and let the afternoon go however it goes. You can hear the espresso machine, the door, and not much else.

The window seat has an outlet.

Good wifi and the good kind of quiet. Whole mornings get done here. The staff will keep the coffee coming and leave you alone otherwise.

  • Free wifi
  • Outlets at most tables
  • Doors open at 6 on weekdays

Nobody will ask what you're reading.

The armchair under the lamp is the good one. Get there early.

Bring them.

There's a little corner with books and blocks where the small ones can be small. You get to finish a coffee while it's still hot, which is rarer than it should be.

Out front, when the weather's right.

A few tables on the sidewalk. Dogs welcome. Sun on your face before the day gets going.

What's good.

The lattes are the reason people drive past three other coffee places to get here. Seasonal drinks change with the weather, so ask what's new.

Signature lattes

  • Brown sugar cinnamonThe one everybody orders first.
  • TiramisuEspresso, mascarpone, cocoa. Dessert, technically.
  • Banana crèmeSweeter than it sounds. Better than it sounds.
  • MatchaHot or iced, with whatever milk you like.
  • Cold brewSteeped overnight. Strong, smooth, no sugar needed.

And the usual

  • Espresso
  • Cappuccino
  • Drip coffee
  • Chai
  • Hot chocolate
  • Tea

From the case

  • MuffinsBlueberry, banana nut, whatever came out of the oven this morning.
  • CroissantsPlain, chocolate, almond when we have them.
  • Scones & cookiesThe chocolate chip cookie is bigger than it needs to be.
  • Bagels & breakfast bitesSomething to go with the coffee if you're staying.

Prices and the full board are at the counter. Order ahead for pickup, or just walk in.

Meet someone here.

No music too loud, nobody hovering. Two cups, a window, and enough quiet to actually talk. The afternoon light does most of the work.

Regulars. That's the word.

Same faces most mornings. The owners know the orders, and usually the names. It's a small place off a big road, and it feels like it belongs to the people who keep coming back.

  • Quality coffee, always consistent.
  • The owners are so sweet and so appreciative of your business.
  • A friendly place to slow down for a moment.
