Our Story

From Main Street,
with care.

The Caffeine Haven opened its doors on Main Street in August 2024 — and quickly became the kind of place Toms River didn't know it was missing. Exposed brick, reclaimed wood, soft indie music humming underneath the conversation. Mornings start quiet here; by 10:30 the tables fill with regulars, laptops, and the occasional stroller. We opened a second haven in Howell not long after, for the same reason we opened the first: good coffee deserves a good place to drink it.
