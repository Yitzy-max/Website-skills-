Our Story

Some places you visit. This one you settle into.

The Caffeine Haven started with a simple idea — a room quiet enough to actually think in. Warm light, soft music, a table by the window that's yours for as long as you need it.

Bring your laptop and the morning disappears. Bring a book and a flat white and it's the best hour of your week. Bring the kids — there's a corner of toys waiting — or bring a date and let the espresso do the talking.
